G-ROCK STORY TIMELINE

REAL FOLKLORE OF THE ROCK

THE MAPS OF GIBRALTAR AND THE MONKEYS: One of the longstanding legends associated with Gibraltar concerns the maps of Gibraltar and the Barbary macaques. According to the traditional story, the presence of the monkeys became connected with the belief that Britain would retain Gibraltar as long as monkeys remained on the Rock. The legend is often expressed through the idea that if the monkeys disappeared from Gibraltar, Britain would eventually lose Gibraltar.

POSSIBLE ORIGIN — A JOKE ON THE SPANISH SIDE: According to one traditional explanation, the legend may have begun as a joke on the Spanish side of Gibraltar. The joke referred humorously to the British possession and connected the continued presence of Britain with the continued presence of the monkeys. Archive Note: Treated as a traditional folkloric explanation, not established historical fact.
