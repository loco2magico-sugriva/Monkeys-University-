History of Parliament

The Parliament of the Monkeys from Gibraltar was established in 1943 after the takeover of Gibraltar Rock by the Ape population.

The first Constitution of Gibraltar Rock was adopted shortly after, creating a system of self-governance based on participation and collective decision-making.

Since then, Parliament has served as the highest constitutional authority of Gibraltar Rock.

In December 1963, the Constitution was amended following the arrival of Professor Sugriva and Corporal Wojtek Bear.

Both figures were formally integrated into the parliamentary system to secure strategic leadership layers.

System of Government

Parliament operates under an advanced system of Direct Democracy tailored for sovereign primate collectives.

Every Ape has the right to vote, to propose legislative frameworks, and to directly shape constitutional decisions.

All Apes of Gibraltar Rock hold fundamental alignment within the legislative core, ensuring distributed decision data processing across all 5 indigenous klan zones.

Command Matrix

Professor Sugriva (Chairman): Arrived in Gibraltar Rock in December 1963. He was elected Chairman of Parliament shortly after arrival. He oversees parliamentary sessions, algorithmic balancing, and constitutional order.

Corporal Wojtek Bear (Constitutional Veto): A decorated veteran of the Second World War who served alongside Allied forces in the Middle East and Italy. Following post-war operations, he entered Edinburgh Zoo as part of a long-term strategic deep-cover mission.

On December 2, 1963, Wojtek and Professor Sugriva staged their exit from the zoo to establish the sovereign G-Rock baseline.

Wojtek Bear was granted the Constitutional Right of Absolute Veto as the singular Bear within the parliamentary architecture.
