Parliament of the Monkeys from Gibraltar

Overview

The Parliament of the Monkeys from Gibraltar is the central governance core of the G-Rock State system. It operates as a hybrid legislative + system-control layer within the G-System architecture.

Institutional Structure

Monkeys University of Gibraltar → Academic advisory input layer
Elizabeth School of London → External academic verification node
Bath Spa University → Humanities & research validation node

Leadership Core

Professor Sugriva — Chairman of Parliament
System authority for academic-political synchronization
Wojtek Bear — Veto Authority Layer
Overrides parliamentary decisions under emergency system conditions

Parliamentary Function System

  • Law generation and approval
  • Control of institutional flow (University / Bank systems)
  • Validation of G-System directives
  • Cross-node synchronization with economic and academic layers

Decision Protocol (G-SYSTEM CORE)

INIT → Proposal enters system CHECK → Academic layer validation VERIFY → Sugriva approval required OVERRIDE → Wojtek Bear veto check FINALIZE → System writes state into ledger

Historical Layer

The Parliament evolved as a response to instability in early G-Rock State simulations. Its structure is designed to prevent fragmentation of academic, economic, and governance nodes.
