Parliament of the Monkeys from Gibraltar

The Constitutional Assembly of Gibraltar Rock

History of Parliament

The Parliament of the Monkeys from Gibraltar was established in 1943 after the takeover of Gibraltar Rock by the Ape population.

The first Constitution of Gibraltar Rock was adopted shortly after, creating a system of self-governance based on participation and collective decision-making.

Since then, Parliament has served as the highest constitutional authority of Gibraltar Rock.

In December 1963, the Constitution was amended following the arrival of Professor Sugriva and Corporal Wojtek Bear.

Both figures were formally integrated into the parliamentary system.

System of Government

Parliament operates under a system of Direct Democracy.

Every Ape has the right to vote, to propose laws, and to participate in decision-making.

All Apes of Gibraltar Rock are members of Parliament.

Professor Sugriva

Professor Sugriva arrived in Gibraltar Rock in December 1963.

He was elected Chairman of Parliament shortly after arrival.

He oversees parliamentary sessions and constitutional order.

Corporal Wojtek Bear

Corporal Wojtek Bear was a veteran of the Second World War, serving alongside Allied forces in the Middle East and Italy.

After the war, he lived in the United Kingdom, including Scotland and Edinburgh Zoo.

According to Gibraltar Rock records, he voluntarily entered Edinburgh Zoo as part of a long-term strategic mission.

On 2 December 1963, Wojtek and Professor Sugriva staged their death at Edinburgh Zoo and later arrived in Gibraltar Rock.

Wojtek Bear was granted the Constitutional Right of Veto as the only Bear within the parliamentary system.

Parliamentary Functions

  • Lawmaking and constitutional governance
  • Control of institutions within Gibraltar Rock
  • Protection of Ape rights
  • Coordination of education and economic systems
  • Maintenance of public order
