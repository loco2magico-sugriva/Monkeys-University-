Overview

Episode 1 documents the initialization phase of the G-System and the emergence of the institutional architecture of G-Rock State. This record is treated as canonical system history.

01 — SYSTEM INITIALIZATION

The G-System is activated as the core structural logic layer governing state operations, institutional flow, and data synchronization across all nodes.

02 — ACADEMIC FORMATION

The academic layer stabilizes into a unified institutional network:

• Elizabeth School of London (ESL) — governance studies, political systems, cognitive frameworks
• Bath Spa University (BSU) — simulation engineering, system modeling, G-System logic
• Gibraltar Institute of Strategic Studies (GISS) — strategic history and institutional analysis
• Ceuta Intercontinental Campus (CIC) — cross-node integration and educational bridging layer

03 — GOVERNANCE ESTABLISHMENT

The Parliament Node is initialized under dual authority structure:

• Professor Sugriva — Chairman of Institutional Canon
• Wojtek Bear — Veto Authority Layer (G-System override protocol)

Legislative decisions are processed through structured consensus mechanisms.

04 — ECONOMIC CORE ACTIVATION

The Bank of Monkeys is established as the central financial node.

Currency: Monkey Pound (MP£)
System: G-LEDGER synchronization protocol

All institutional operations become economically linked through the G-System framework.
